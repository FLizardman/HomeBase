# Homebase Privacy Policy

*Effective October 19, 2026*

Homebase is a private app run by one household for its own members. It isn't offered to the public: only people the
household adds can use it. This page explains what it does with your Google data.

## What it accesses

- Connecting Gmail is each person's own choice. Nothing is read until you connect.
- It asks only for **read-only** access to Gmail (`gmail.readonly`). It can't send, delete, label or change any of
  your mail.

## How it uses your mail

- New mail is read about every 30 minutes, to keep your packages, bills, renewals and appointments up to date in
  Homebase.
- To understand an email, its text is sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) through the household's own Amazon Web
  Services account. That's done only to answer that one request.

## What it keeps

- For each email: its sender, subject and date, and what it found (for example a tracking number, or a bill's amount
  and due date). The email itself isn't kept.
- What it keeps is private to you: other people in the household, the app's admins included, can't see it.
- It's stored on the household's own server and in that server's encrypted backup.

## What it never does

- Your data isn't sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.
- It isn't used to train AI models.
- No person reads your mail.

## Stopping it

- **Disconnect** in Homebase (Assistant, then Settings) stops all reading and revokes Homebase's access at Google. You
  can also remove the access any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- Ask the household's admin to delete what was kept from your mail, and it will be deleted.

## Google API Services

Homebase's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

Questions: the support email shown on Google's Homebase sign-in screen.
